# Inbox Hero

Share the folders you choose with your Inbox Hero account, so email drafts and answers can use your notes.

- You choose the folders. Sync is one way and your notes are never changed.
- Notes that look like they hold passwords, keys, card numbers or seed phrases are left out.
- Emails you send to Obsidian from Inbox Hero are saved as new notes in the folder "Inbox Hero".

Needs an Inbox Hero account (https://joininboxhero.com). Network use: the plugin talks only to https://joininboxhero.com, to upload the notes you share and to fetch emails you sent to your vault.
